Inspired by Richard Feynman’s words, “There’s plenty of room at the bottom,” I was driven to explore materials at the molecular level, believing it is the key to their understanding and enhancement. I joined this research group in 2026 after earning a BSc in Chemistry from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Ghana. My research at UNL aims to apply computational and theoretical modeling approaches to understand the fundamental behaviors of materials in electrochemical interfaces.
